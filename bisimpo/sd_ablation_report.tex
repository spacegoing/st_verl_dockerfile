\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Single-domain (nemogym\_math) ablation of BSPO's three sd-applicable
variants across a $(\delta,\, \lambda_{\mathrm{Tj}})$ grid. All runs:
40Bra-40B, 4-node profile (PP=2, EP=8, TP=1), 120 training steps
(\texttt{test\_freq=10} $\Rightarrow$ 13 val checkpoints per run),
\texttt{train\_batch\_size=128}, \texttt{ppo\_mini\_batch\_size=128},
\texttt{rollout.n=16}, \texttt{ppo\_epochs=2}, GRPO advantage, Gaussian
linear curriculum (\texttt{initial\_mean=0.8} $\to$
\texttt{final\_mean=0.2} over 300 curriculum steps), hard-eval set =
\{BeyondAIME, AIME2025, OlympiadBench\}. Data extracted from wandb
offline stores via \texttt{bisimpo/wandb\_parse\_offline.py} into CSVs
under \texttt{bisimpo/wandb\_csvs/}.
\end{abstract}

%==============================================================================
\section{Meaningful-run filter}
%==============================================================================

Of the 66 wandb-extracted offline runs on the B300 dev cluster, runs
were included in the analysis iff they met all four criteria:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Launch commit is post-reset.} The clean-reset baseline
  was \texttt{verl@7dc6b1fa}. Runs on earlier commits (notably
  \texttt{96fee429}, which produced cbsp101/cbsp103/cbsp104 Phase-1
  runs before the mdplan-v1 bug audit) are excluded because the
  sd-vs-md mix during those commits cannot be ruled out as a
  confounder.
\item \textbf{Three-eval validation.} CSV must contain
  \texttt{val-core/beyondaime/acc/mean@1},
  \texttt{val-core/aime2025/acc/mean@1}, and
  \texttt{val-core/olympiadbench/acc/mean@1} columns with at least
  one non-null row each.
\item \textbf{More than 80 training steps} reached. Eliminates the
  queued-but-unscheduled slots (cbsp204/205/305--308 at step $\leq
  10$), debug smokes (cdbg5, cdbgbspo), and the aborted early-session
  restarts of cbsp201/202/301/302 at $\texttt{7dc6b1fa}$ which died
  at step 35.
\item \textbf{CSV parses cleanly} with matched train and val steps.
  Runs that wrote val metrics to wandb but had no history records
  parse (e.g.\ \texttt{cbsp-v5-l1e2} at step 0) are excluded.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent Twelve runs pass. Three runs pass all criteria except
\#1 (the cbsp10X Phase-1 triplet) and are listed separately in an
appendix for completeness.

\section{Combo naming convention}
%==============================================================================

Combo ids follow the pattern \texttt{cbspXYZ} where:

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{X} (hundreds) encodes the BSPO variant.
\item \texttt{YZ} (tens+units) encodes a hyperparameter slot; no
  intrinsic meaning. Per-run $\{\delta, \lambda_{\mathrm{Tj}}\}$ come
  from a CLI override passed through
  \texttt{bspo\_scripts/submit\_bspo.sh}; the combo's yaml entry fixes
  only the non-hparam knobs (\texttt{ppo\_epochs, total\_training\_steps,
  domains, curriculum\_total\_steps, save\_freq}).
\end{itemize}

\noindent Variant mapping (consistent with the md naming in
\texttt{bspo\_algorithm.tex}):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cll>{\ttfamily}ll}
\toprule
hundreds & md name              & sd objective $\ell_\tau$                                 & loss\_mode & verl config \\
\midrule
2XX      & V1 simplest          & $\Delta_\tau^{(\mathrm{reg})}\hat A_\tau$                & bspo       & \texttt{bspo\_variant=simplest} \\
3XX      & V4 w\_penalty        & $\Delta_\tau^{(\mathrm{W})}\hat A_\tau - \lambda\,\mathrm{pen}_\tau$ & bspo & \texttt{bspo\_variant=w\_penalty} \\
4XX      & V5 w\_penalty\_only  & $s_\tau\hat A_\tau - \lambda\,\mathrm{pen}_\tau$         & bspo       & \texttt{bspo\_variant=w\_penalty\_only} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent where
\begin{align*}
s_\tau^\pm                   &= \tfrac{1}{|\tau|}\sum_t \max(\pm(r_t - 1),\,0),\qquad s_\tau = s_\tau^+ - s_\tau^- \\
\Delta_\tau^{(\mathrm{reg})} &= \mathrm{clip}(s_\tau^+, \delta) - \mathrm{clip}(s_\tau^-, \delta) \\
\Delta_\tau^{(\mathrm{W})}   &= \mathrm{sgn}(s_\tau)\cdot \mathrm{clip}\bigl(\min(s_\tau^+, s_\tau^-),\, \delta\bigr) \\
\mathrm{pen}_\tau            &= \max\bigl(0,\ \min(s_\tau^+, s_\tau^-)/\delta - 1\bigr) \\
r_t &= \exp(\log\pi_\theta - \log\pi_{\mathrm{old}})_t
\end{align*}

\section{Analysis}
%==============================================================================

\subsection{V1 is the only variant with a real upward signal}

cbsp203 hits AIME2025 $= 0.83$ at step 80 vs.\ step-0 baseline 0.67
($+0.16$ absolute). cbsp201 peaks at step 80, $0.59/0.77/0.70$ vs.\
baseline $0.52/0.70/0.65$ ($+0.07/+0.07/+0.05$). cbsp202 peaks at step
50, $0.58/0.70/0.70$ vs.\ baseline $0.49/0.70/0.73$ ($+0.09/0/-0.03$).

No V4 or V5 run crosses the step-0 baseline on AIME2025 at any
checkpoint. V5 peak points (e.g.\ cbsp401 step 20: $0.53/0.73/0.72$
vs.\ baseline $0.49/0.73/0.72$) are within $\pm 0.04$ of start.

\section{Recommendations}
%==============================================================================

\subsection{Current winner: V5, $\delta=3\text{e-}4$, $\lambda=1\text{e-}2$}

Reproduced across cbsp401 and cbsp406. End-of-training within sampling
noise of step-0 baseline. This is the only \emph{stable} run. Does
not \emph{improve} the base model.

\subsection{Highest-value frontier: capture V1's peak without the crash}

cbsp203 demonstrates that V1 achieves genuine learning: $+0.16$ AIME2025 at
step 80 is above the benchmark's sampling noise band ($\approx 0.07$
on a 30-sample eval). V1's late collapse is the blocker. Four
orthogonal hypotheses for the collapse are queued for the B200 cluster
(\texttt{bisimpo/b200\_8runs\_proposal.md}): tighter PPO clip, KL
anchor, entropy regularisation, half lr. A fifth worth adding is
\texttt{curriculum\_total\_steps=600} to directly test the
curriculum-drift hypothesis.

\subsection{De-prioritise V4}

V4 is strictly dominated at every tested $(\delta, \lambda)$.
Remaining queued V4 slots (cbsp305--cbsp309) should be repurposed as
additional V1 or V5 probes.

\subsection{V5 next directions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{$\delta=1\text{e-}4$} (tighter than any tested) ---
  V5's only strong monotone axis is $\delta$; tighter might extend
  stability further or reveal a plateau.
\item \textbf{$\lambda=1\text{e-}3$} at $\delta=3\text{e-}4$ ---
  lighter penalty is unexplored; tests whether the current sweet spot
  is a floor.
\item \textbf{Seed repeat of cbsp406} --- cbsp401 and cbsp406 differ
  on AIME2025 end-value by $\pm 0.06$ ($-0.20$ vs $-0.30$), which is
  borderline significant on a 30-sample benchmark. A third replicate
  would nail down whether that's noise or drift.
\end{itemize}
