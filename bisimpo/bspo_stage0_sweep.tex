\section{Overview}

Stage 0 covers two independent sweeps: a single-domain sweep focused on
objective variant + two targeted secondary axes, and a multi-domain sweep
covering only the five objectives.

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}
\begin{tabular}{l l l}
\toprule
block & what is swept & \# runs \\
\midrule
Single-domain --- obj baseline  & V1, V2, V3, V4, V5 at $(S_1, A_1, T_1, L_3)$ & 5 \\
Single-domain --- sign on V4    & V4 with $S_2$, $S_3$                         & 2 \\
Single-domain --- agg on V5     & V5 with $A_2$ (fiberPO residue)              & 1 \\
\midrule
Multi-domain --- obj baseline   & V1, V2, V3, V4, V5                           & 5 \\
\bottomrule
& \textbf{total}                                                               & \textbf{13} \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Defaults per \texttt{bspo\_sweep\_plan.tex}: $S_1 = $\verb|.detach()|;
$A_1 = $ per-traj broadcast with \verb|seq-mean-token-mean|;
$T_1 = (1/|\tau|)\sum_t(\cdot)$; $L_3 = $\verb|seq-mean-token-mean|.

Hyperparameters shared across all 13 runs: $\delta = 3\!\times\!10^{-4}$,
$\lambda_{\text{Tj}} = 10^{-3}$, $\lambda_{\text{Grp}} = 10^{-3}$,
$\lambda_{\text D} = 1$, \verb|ppo_epochs=2|, \verb|total_training_steps=120|,
\verb|test_freq=10|, \verb|val_before_train=true|, 4-node C11 profile.
Eval set: the 230-row hard parquet (BeyondAIME 100 + AIME25 30 +
OlympiadBench 100), routed through \verb|nemogym_server|.

\section{Code additions required before launch}

Two of the five objective variants (V2, V3) and the two secondary-axis
options (S2/S3 signs, A2 aggregation) are not yet in
\verb|compute_policy_loss_bspo|. The sweep presupposes these minimal
additions.

\subsection*{(i) Group-/domain-level aggregation of $s_z^\pm$ and $\hat A_z$}

\begin{lstlisting}
# inputs: s_pos (B,), s_neg (B,), A_tau (B,)
# non-tensor batch provides:
#   group_idx  (B,) int64   -- prompt id
#   domain_idx (B,) int64   -- data_source id (0 in single-domain)

from torch_scatter import scatter_mean  # or implement with index_add + counts

# --- group level ---
s_g_pos = scatter_mean(torch.clamp(s_pos - s_neg, min=0),
                       group_idx, dim=0)                # (n_groups,)
s_g_neg = scatter_mean(torch.clamp(s_neg - s_pos, min=0),
                       group_idx, dim=0)
A_g     = scatter_mean(A_tau, group_idx, dim=0)          # (n_groups,)
# broadcast back to per-tau: s_g_pos_tau = s_g_pos[group_idx], etc.

# --- domain level ---
# single-domain: domain_idx is all zeros, so these collapse trivially
s_dom_pos = scatter_mean(torch.clamp(s_g_pos - s_g_neg, min=0),
                          domain_g_idx, dim=0)          # (n_domains,)
s_dom_neg = scatter_mean(torch.clamp(s_g_neg - s_g_pos, min=0),
                          domain_g_idx, dim=0)
A_dom     = scatter_mean(A_g, domain_g_idx, dim=0)
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection*{(ii) V2 and V3 variant branches}

\begin{lstlisting}
# within compute_policy_loss_bspo, after s_g_*, s_dom_*, A_g, A_dom are ready

delta_g_reg   = torch.clamp(s_g_pos_tau, max=delta_g_tau) \
                - torch.clamp(s_g_neg_tau, max=delta_g_tau)
delta_g_w     = torch.sign(s_g_pos_tau - s_g_neg_tau).detach() \
                * torch.clamp(torch.minimum(s_g_pos_tau, s_g_neg_tau),
                              max=delta_g_tau)
# similarly delta_dom_reg, delta_dom_w at domain level
# pen_g, pen_dom analogous to pen_tau

if variant == "hierarchy":            # V2
    per_traj = (delta_reg - delta_g_reg) * A_tau
elif variant == "strict_wasserstein": # V3
    per_traj = (delta_dom_w  * A_dom
              + delta_g_w    * A_g
              + delta_w      * A_tau)
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection*{(iii) S2 and S3 sign treatments}

Per \texttt{bspo\_sweep\_plan.tex}~\S3. S2 is a mask-and-add; S3 is a
\verb|torch.autograd.Function| subclass.

\subsection*{(iv) A2 fiberPO residue aggregation}

Per \texttt{bspo\_sweep\_plan.tex}~\S4. Replaces the broadcast-then-agg
step; per-token residue added inside the per-trajectory scalar.

\subsection*{(v) New \texttt{bspo\_variant} values and \texttt{bspo\_sign\_mode}/\texttt{bspo\_agg\_mode} config fields}

Add to \verb|verl/verl/workers/config/actor.py| and
\verb|verl/verl/trainer/config/actor/actor.yaml|:
\begin{lstlisting}
# actor.yaml
bspo_variant: simplest   # simplest | hierarchy | strict_wasserstein | w_penalty | w_penalty_only
bspo_sign_mode: detach   # detach | near_fault_bias | case_patch
bspo_agg_mode: broadcast # broadcast | fiberpo_residue
bspo_sign_eps_dir: 1.0e-2      # 0.01; 1e-3 too weak to break sign ties at fault
bspo_sign_eps_patch: 3.0e-4
bspo_sign_eps_fault: 3.0e-5   # 0.1 * delta
bspo_agg_delta_tok: 0.2       # gsPO per-token scale
\end{lstlisting}

\paragraph{Rationale for the secondary axes.}
$S_2, S_3$ on V4 and not on V5: V4 uses
$\Delta_\tau^{(W)}\hat A_\tau$ whose $\sign$ factor is exactly the term
the sign-gradient sweep is designed to probe. V5's main term is
$s_\tau\hat A_\tau$, which already has full gradient flow through
$s_\tau$ and does not require a sign handler. Conversely, the fiberPO
residue decomposition targets the $s_\tau\hat A_\tau$ term directly
(it replaces the trajectory mean by mean + per-token residue); V5 is
the cleanest place to apply it, and V4's additional clip structure
would confound the residue contribution.

\paragraph{Launch.}
\verb|bash bisimpo/launch_stage0_sd.sh|. Script submits all 8 in
sequence, 4 concurrent on the C11 profile ($8\cdot 4 = 32$ nodes; we
have $28$, so 7 run concurrently and 1 queues).

#!/usr/bin/env bash

\section{Wall-clock budget}

At $\sim 20$h per run on the 4-node C11 profile and $28$ usable nodes:

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{l c c c l}
\toprule
block & runs & nodes & concurrent & wall clock \\
\midrule
Single-domain                            & 8 & $8\cdot 4 = 32$ & 7 & $\lceil 8/7\rceil\cdot 20 = 40$h \\
Multi-domain (4-node C11)                & 5 & $5\cdot 4 = 20$ & 5 & $20$h \\
Combined if parallel\footnote{capacity permitting; single-dom + multi-dom together need $32+20=52$ nodes, exceeding the 28 available, so runs serialise.}
                                         & 13 & -- & 7 & $\lceil 13/7\rceil\cdot 20 = 40$h \\
Combined if serialised                   & 13 & -- & 7 & $40 + 20 = 60$h \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Recommended: launch single-domain first (8 runs, 40h). When $\geq 4$
single-domain nodes free up, start multi-domain (5 runs, 20h). Total
elapsed: $\sim 50$h.

\section{Decision metric and what follows}

Same metric as \texttt{bspo\_sweep\_plan.tex}: mean of final-step
\verb|val-core/{beyondaime, aime2025, olympiadbench}/acc/mean@1|, with
stability tie-breakers if within $1$pp.

\paragraph{Next stage depends on Stage-0 outcome.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{If V4 single-domain winner.} Pick the best of
\verb|{cbsp104, cbsp114, cbsp115}| as the V4 configuration. Schedule
Phase 2 hparam sweep on $\delta\in\{1,3,10\}\times 10^{-4}$,
$\lambda_{\text{Tj}}\in\{1,3,10\}\times 10^{-3}$ (9-run grid). Combos
\verb|cbsp301..cbsp309|.
\item \textbf{If V5 single-domain winner.} Pick between
\verb|cbsp105| and \verb|cbsp125|. Schedule Phase 2 sweep on
$\delta$ and $\lambda_{\text{Tj}}$ with the chosen aggregation. Combos
\verb|cbsp401..cbsp409|.
\item \textbf{If V1 or V2 wins.} Schedule a $\delta$-only sweep
($3$--$5$ runs).
\item \textbf{If V3 wins.} Schedule a $\delta_{\text{D}}$,
$\delta_{\operatorname{Grp}}$, $\delta_{\operatorname{Tj}}$ three-axis
reduced sweep ($\sim 9$ runs at balanced scales).
\item \textbf{Multi-domain winner compared against single-domain winner.}
Guide Phase 2 scope: if rank orders differ significantly, Phase 2 runs
both single-domain (specialist) and multi-domain (generalist) grids; if
they agree, Phase 2 runs only on the single-domain winner's grid and the
multi-domain generalisation is validated at the end.
\end{itemize}
